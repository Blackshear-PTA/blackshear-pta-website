Blackshear is celebrating 135 years, and KUT's ATXplained confirmed what we
suspected: it is Austin's oldest continuously operating elementary school.

The school opened in 1891 and has been part of this neighbourhood ever since,
through every change East Austin has been through. KVUE covered the celebration
as well.

It is worth saying out loud what that means. Children have been walking through
those doors for longer than anyone reading this has been alive, and the PTA's
job is to make sure that keeps being true.
