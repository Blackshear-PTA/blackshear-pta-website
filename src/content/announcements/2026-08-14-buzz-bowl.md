date: 2026-08-14

Our own take on Battle of the Books is back, and the PTA is sponsoring it again
this year.

Here is how it works. Parent volunteers read with 3rd, 4th and 5th graders in
the weeks beforehand. Students then compete in a trivia showdown on the books
they have read, and everyone celebrates afterwards with ice cream and donuts.

It is one of the easiest things to volunteer for. You read with kids, which is
the good part, and you do not need any experience to do it.

Watch for sign-ups closer to the date.
